Continuation Guide

Wondering where the anime or manga ends and where the light novel picks up? This guide maps each season to the corresponding light novel volumes so you can jump straight into the books without spoilers.

Heads-up: Season boundaries are approximate — some episodes blend volumes or reorder chapters. Everything here is unofficial, fan-curated information. For authoritative details, check the Fandom wiki.

Where to continue after the anime

Finished Season 1 → start at Light Novel Volume 5. Season 1 adapts roughly Volumes 1–4, stopping just before Volume 4's epilogue.

Finished Season 2 → start at Light Novel Volume 7. Season 2 adapts roughly Volumes 5–6, plus parts of Volume 7.

Finished Season 3 → start at Light Novel Volume 10. Season 3 adapts roughly Volumes 7–9, ending with the events of Volume 9.

Caught up with Season 4 → the newest episodes have reached Light Novel Volume 11. To read ahead, start at Volume 12, "The Eve of War".

Manga alignment

The main manga by Taiki Kawakami is ongoing. As of mid-2026 it has reached chapter 144 (compiled manga volume 32), which places it roughly halfway through Light Novel Volume 11 — so the manga currently sits behind the anime's latest episodes. Roughly, the first season covers about the first 52 manga chapters. For chapter-level details see the Fandom Chapters page.

Status of each medium

  • Light novel: Complete — 23 volumes, the final volume released on November 29, 2025. Light novel
  • Manga: Ongoing — chapter 144 (July 2026). Manga
  • Anime: Season 4 (episodes 73–96) is airing across 2025–26, with more cours planned for spring 2027 covering the Empire arc (Volume 12+). Anime
